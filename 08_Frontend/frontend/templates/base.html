How to Use GestureConnect
Text Input

Type any sentence in Marathi (Devanagari script) or English. The NLP engine normalizes tokens, resolves lexical synonyms, and maps to canonical sign IDs. Press Ctrl+Enter to translate instantly.

Microphone Speech Input

Click the microphone icon and speak clearly into your mic. The Web Speech API recognizes speech in real-time, displays the transcript, and automatically launches the translation pipeline.

3D Avatar Sign Playback

Each translated word triggers a smooth 3D sign animation clip rendered from Blender. Use the player controls to pause, replay, change speed, or step forward/back through the sentence signs.

Fingerspelling Fallback

Words outside the predefined vocabulary are never discarded. They are decomposed into character letters and fingerspelled so the signer can understand any proper noun or new term.
