Architectural Highlights

Strictly designed following the GestureConnect research paper methodology with clean separation between linguistic layers and 3D visual assets.

Bilingual Speech & Text

Accepts Marathi text, English text, and Marathi speech via microphone. Both modalities converge into a common normalized text representation.

5-Level Linguistic Mapping

Multi-priority matching: Phrase-level → Exact word-level → Normalized-form → Lexical synonyms → Canonical Sign IDs.

Fingerspelling Fallback

Out-of-vocabulary words are never discarded. Words decompose into Devanagari and Latin alphabet animation sequences seamlessly.

3D Avatar Sign Animations

Pre-rendered Blender MP4 sign clips linked by Canonical Sign IDs. Sequential playback delivers smooth sentence-level Marathi Sign Language output.

Decoupled Sign Database

Structured sign vocabulary stores linguistic and biomechanical metadata. Easily expandable via Django Admin without touching translation logic.

Real-time Performance

Lightweight in-memory token lookup, deterministic response latency, and verifiable evaluation metrics for academic reporting.
